To the tune of 'Santiana'

(Chorus)
We'll cast the hawser, set our course
"For Crew and For Glory!"
We bare our Flag, show no remorse
Oh free ones, never shall we die!

(Verse 1)
O Needle, pierce the void, and cry
"For Crew and For Glory!"
Through the Black, we devils fly
Oh free ones, never shall we die!

[Chorus]
(Verse 2)
Oh! Charge the guns, grapplers away!
For Crew and For Glory!
We scuttle their bones, we win the day
Oh free ones, never shall we die!


[Chorus]
(Verse 3)
Oh, Saanda Ness, she's cunning and cold, 
For Crew and For Glory!
What deeds she did, have oft been told
Oh free ones, never shall she die!

[Chorus]
(Verse 4)
Smeegibb, Horde o' the Stars, they're named
For Crew and for Glory!
Dreaded swarm, like a tidal wave
Oh free ones, never shall they die!


[Chorus]
(Verse 5)
'Twas a heist o' daring and terror that night
For Crew and for Glory!
Stole ourselves the Dark Gods' Light
Oh free ones, never shall we die!

[Chorus]
(Verse 6)
A row on decks, a shot was fired
For Crew and for Glory!
The cost was the world o' the Ytterites
Oh free ones, never shall they die!


[Chorus]
(Verse 7)
For once in her life, our Queen did mourn,
For Crew and for Glory!
She cached her gold out 'mongst the moons,
Oh free ones, never shall it die!

[Chorus]
(Verse 8)
Oh Captain Ness, laid down her sword
Her pride, and her story
She cast her body off and swore,
"Oh free ones, never shall I die."
